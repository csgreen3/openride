Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026.

The short version

OpenRide does not collect, send or sell any personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you record stays on your device.

What is stored, and where

  • Your settings (weight, FTP, 5 km time, units) and training plan progress: in the app's storage on your device.
  • Session summaries for history, ghost racing and the weekly rings: in the app's storage on your device.
  • Activity files (.tcx) and share card images (.png): saved to your device when a session ends. They go somewhere else only if you share them yourself, for example to Strava.

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. Past sessions can also be deleted in the app.

Bluetooth

OpenRide uses Bluetooth only to connect to sensors you choose: a smart trainer, treadmill, foot pod, heart rate strap, Zwift Click, or the Swimcord app on your Garmin watch. Readings from them are used live and in your saved sessions on the device. Android lists this as location permission on older versions; OpenRide never reads your location.

Network

The app makes one kind of internet request: checking this website's latest.json for a newer version. That request carries no personal data. GitHub, which hosts this site, may keep standard server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

OpenRide is not aimed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
